The practical details.

What counts toward the two hours?

Investigation, implementation, testing, communication about a change and deployment count toward the monthly two-hour fix allowance. Routine monthly checks and the standard report are separate inclusions. Work is prioritized together. Unused time does not roll over. No extra paid work is started without written agreement.

When will you respond?

Our email response target is within two business days, Monday to Friday excluding Japanese public holidays. This is a response target, not a guaranteed resolution time or an emergency service. Completion dates are agreed for the individual task.

How do payments, cancellation and refunds work?

The service fee is US$199 per month, including any tax collected by Nipporia on this fee. Hosting, domain and third-party charges remain yours. The first month is paid in advance after the scope is accepted. Any recurring billing requires your separate authorization. Email info@nipporia.com before your next renewal to cancel the next period; service remains available for the paid period. An unused first month may be cancelled for a full refund before work starts. After work starts, no partial-month refund is normally offered, except where legally required or where we cannot supply the agreed service. All details are confirmed in the written service agreement before payment.

How is access handled?

Use separate, least-privilege accounts or repository invitations. Never send passwords, API keys or payment details in an inquiry. Changes require an agreed backup or rollback path and release approval. No production form submissions, paid test orders or customer communications are made without explicit permission. Confidential code or customer data is not shared with third-party AI tools without the client's agreement.

Does this change another Nipporia package?

No. Website Care is a separate, limited-scope offer. Existing project warranties, included support periods and contracted prices remain unchanged. This page accepts inquiries, not automatic orders; the Website Care-specific agreement must be accepted before any service or billing begins.
